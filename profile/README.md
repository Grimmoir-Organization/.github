## About

**Grimmoir Organization** is an independent organization built around the creation, development, maintenance, and publication of projects.

There is no fixed technology stack, field, or project category that defines the organization.

Projects may exist as independent works, collaborative efforts, experiments, long-term systems, or maintained releases under the Grimmoir Organization.

---

## Project Model

Repositories within Grimmoir Organization may follow different development and ownership models.

A project may be:

- developed directly under Grimmoir Organization;
- maintained independently within the organization;
- attributed to its individual author or maintainer;
- developed collaboratively;
- experimental, archived, private, or publicly maintained.

Each repository is responsible for documenting its own purpose, status, maintainers, licensing, and contribution requirements where applicable.

---

## Attribution

Grimmoir Organization does not require every project to use the same public attribution.

Depending on the repository, a project may be presented as:

> **A Grimmoir Organization Project**

or

> **Developed under Grimmoir Organization**

or may identify its individual author or maintainer directly.

Repository-level documentation remains the authoritative source for project-specific attribution.

---

## Repository Status

Projects may use simple lifecycle indicators where useful:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Active` | Under active development or maintenance |
| `Experimental` | Exploring an idea, architecture, or implementation |
| `Stable` | Considered usable for its documented purpose |
| `Maintenance` | Receiving fixes or limited updates |
| `Archived` | No longer actively maintained |

Individual repositories may use different terminology where appropriate.

---

## Collaboration

Public repositories may accept issues, discussions, suggestions, or pull requests depending on the project.

Before contributing, review the repository's documentation and any project-specific contribution requirements.

Organization-wide community guidelines are maintained in the [`Grimmoir-Organization/.github`](https://github.com/Grimmoir-Organization/.github) repository.

---

## Principles

**Project autonomy**  
Each project may define its own architecture, workflow, attribution, and license.

**Clear documentation**  
A repository should explain what it is, what state it is in, and how it should be used.

**Practical development**  
Structure should serve the project rather than exist only for appearance.

**Room to evolve**  
The organization is intentionally not limited to a predefined field, technology, or type of work.
