# Grimmoir Organization

Grimmoir Organization is an independent organization for developing, maintaining, and publishing projects across different technologies and domains.

Projects may be developed directly under the organization, maintained independently within it, or published as collaborative work.

Each project may define its own purpose, development process, maintainers, contributors, ownership, and licensing terms where applicable.

## Projects

Projects published under Grimmoir Organization may differ significantly in scope, purpose, technology, and development model.

Some projects may be maintained directly under the organization, while others may retain individual authorship or project-specific attribution.

For information about a particular project, refer to its repository documentation.

## Contributions

Contribution policies may differ between projects.

Please review the documentation and contribution guidelines of the relevant repository before submitting issues, suggestions, or pull requests.

## Organization

Grimmoir Organization operates as an independent development organization.
