# Code of Conduct

Grimmoir Organization should be a welcoming place to build, learn, talk, and have fun together. Everyone helps shape that environment through how they treat others.

## How We Treat Each Other

- Be respectful, regardless of someone's background or experience.
- Welcome questions and help people learn without making them feel embarrassed.
- Give useful feedback. Disagree with ideas without attacking the person behind them.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, and availability.
- Give credit for other people's work and contributions.

## Enjoy Being Here

Casual conversations, jokes, and sharing interests are welcome. Get to know the people you build with, celebrate small wins, and support each other when things get difficult.

Keep issues and pull requests focused enough for others to follow. Use designated social or general discussion spaces for longer casual conversations.

Humour should be enjoyable for everyone involved. If someone says a joke or conversation makes them uncomfortable, respect that and stop.

## What Is Not Acceptable

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted abuse, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Pressuring someone to participate, mocking their questions, or repeatedly ignoring their boundaries is also not acceptable.

## When Something Goes Wrong

If a conversation becomes uncomfortable or a problem needs attention, contact a project maintainer or organization owner. Raise sensitive concerns privately where possible.

Maintainers should handle concerns fairly and consider the context. They may ask participants to change their behaviour, remove harmful content, or restrict participation when necessary.

Repository-specific conduct policies may extend or replace this organization-wide default.
