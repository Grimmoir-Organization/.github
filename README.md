## Community

We want members and contributors to enjoy building, learning, and talking with each other. Our [public profile](profile/README.md) explains our culture, and our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) sets expectations for keeping that community welcoming.

## Scope

These files are intentionally general because Grimmoir Organization is not restricted to one technology, field, development model, or project category.

A repository may provide its own policies whenever its requirements differ from these defaults.

## Project Authority

Repository-specific documentation takes precedence over organization-wide defaults for:

- licensing;
- attribution;
- maintainership;
- contribution requirements;
- support expectations;
- release policy;
- security scope.
